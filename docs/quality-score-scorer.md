# Paid Ad Quality Score & Landing Page Checklist Scorer

## What it answers

How would this keyword, ad and landing page likely score on Google's three Quality Score components, and which fixes matter most?

## When to use it

Before launching a new ad group or landing page, or when a keyword shows a Quality Score of 5 or below and you need a structured audit.

## What you need

The target keyword, ad headlines and descriptions (one per line), display path or final URL, the landing page title, H1 and visible body text. Then tick the page experience checklist: load speed, mobile layout, CTA above the fold, trust links, HTTPS without interstitials, original content, ad extensions and ad group theme.

## How it works

**Text checks** reduce the keyword to stemmed words and look for them in:

- headlines (exact phrase in at least one, all words across the set)
- descriptions
- the page title, H1 and the first 600 characters of body copy
- the body (all words plus at least 150 words of substance)
- the URL
- **message match:** at least 35% of the ad's words reappear on the page

**Weighted scoring.** Each check carries a weight from 1 to 4. The tool groups checks into Expected CTR, Ad relevance and Landing page experience, and scores each component from 0 to 100. Components rate Above average at 70 or higher and Below average under 40. The overall estimate combines the components with weights of 39% (CTR), 30% (relevance) and 31% (landing page), scaled to 1 to 10.

## Reading the results

"Fix these first" lists failed checks by weight. Page speed, the keyword in the H1 and message match carry the most weight.

## Limits and cautions

- This is a checklist estimate. Google calculates the real score from auction history you cannot see.
- Stemming catches plurals and common suffixes but not synonyms.
- Expected CTR depends on your competitors' ads, which no checklist can observe.
