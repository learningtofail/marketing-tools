Platform reporting

Enter what each platform reports and the window it used. View share is the portion of that platform's reported conversions that came from view-through (leave 0 for search). A platform with spend and no conversions stays in the table and ranks last.

Replaces the rows above when you press Normalize. Click window, view window and view share are optional and default to 7, 0 and 0.

Target and lag

From your CRM or analytics: days between click and conversion, with counts, from orders old enough to have finished converting. Without it the tool uses a typical ecommerce curve (65% same day, 88% by day 7, 97% by day 28), which is wrong for B2B, automotive, travel and other long sales cycles.
