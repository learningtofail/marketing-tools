# Affiliate Commission & Margin Viability Calculator

## What it answers

What is the highest commission you can pay affiliates and still hit your target margin, and which partner tiers lose money at their current rates?

## When to use it

Designing an affiliate program, renegotiating tiers, or evaluating a partner who asks for a higher rate or a flat bounty.

## What you need

Product economics: retail price, COGS per unit, payment fees (percentage and fixed), shipping and packaging per order, return rate, share of returned units you resell, and any customer discount the affiliate offers. Program rules: target net margin, whether you reverse commission on returned orders, and a flat payout per order to test. Partner tiers: monthly orders and commission rate for each tier (edit the table directly).

## How it works

1. **Net order value** = price times (1 minus affiliate discount)
2. **Kept revenue** = net value times (1 minus return rate)
3. **COGS lost** = COGS times (1 minus return rate times resale share)
4. **Margin before commission** = kept revenue minus COGS lost, payment fees and shipping
5. **Commission base** = net value, or kept revenue when commissions reverse on returns

- **Maximum safe commission** = (margin before commission minus target margin times kept revenue) divided by the commission base
- **Break-even commission** = margin before commission divided by the commission base
- **Maximum flat payout** = the same limit expressed in dollars per order

Each tier is scored at its own rate: viable, below target, or losing money, with a suggested cap.

## Reading the results

The banner turns red when your target margin is unreachable even with zero commission. That points to a pricing or cost problem, not a commission problem. The sensitivity chart shows how quickly margin erodes as the rate rises.

## Limits and cautions

- Per-order economics only. Fixed program costs (network fees, management time, bonuses) sit outside the model.
- Returns vary by partner. Coupon and deal sites often run higher return rates than content partners.
- The model does not judge incrementality. A partner can clear margin while taking credit for sales you would have made anyway.
