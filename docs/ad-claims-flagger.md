# Ad Claims Flagger

## What it answers

Which phrases in your copy make claims you need to prove, qualify or send to legal before launch?

## When to use it

Pre-launch review of ads, landing pages, emails, video scripts and affiliate or influencer briefs.

## What you need

Paste the copy. Nothing else.

## How it works

A pattern library scans the text for claim types, each with a severity level and advice:

| Claim type | Examples | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Ranking superlative | #1, number one, best, industry-leading, award-winning | High |
| Comparative extreme | fastest, cheapest, lowest price, safest | High |
| Guarantee | guaranteed, money-back, 100% satisfaction | High |
| Absolute | risk-free, foolproof, never fails, permanent | High |
| Health or science | clinically proven, doctor recommended, cures, detox | High |
| Results | lose 10 lbs, earn $5,000, guaranteed returns | High |
| Comparative | better than, outperforms, unlike competitors | High |
| Price or offer | save 40%, up to 50% off, free, limited time | Medium |
| Environmental or natural | eco-friendly, recyclable, carbon neutral, all natural | Medium |
| Origin | Made in USA, Made in Canada | Medium |
| Social proof | trusted by 50,000, as seen on | Medium |
| Qualifier | results vary, terms apply, based on, source: | Present (good) |

Overlapping matches resolve to the first and longest. The tool also checks whether an asterisk has matching footnote text.

## Reading the results

The annotated copy highlights high-risk phrases in red, phrases to qualify in amber, and qualifiers found in green. The claims table lists each phrase with the proof or terms needed. Copy the review checklist into your legal ticket.

## Limits and cautions

- This is a screen, not legal advice. It matches phrases and cannot judge whether a claim is true.
- Rules differ by jurisdiction and industry: the FTC and state law in the US, the Competition Bureau and Ad Standards in Canada, the ASA in the UK, plus stricter regimes for health, finance, alcohol and children.
- It reads only English copy.
